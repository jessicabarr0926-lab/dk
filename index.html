Solo travel rules

Uber after dark and when moving between neighborhoods. Confirm the plate before getting in. Keep your passport secured at the hotel unless you need the original. Carry a backup card separately, keep your phone charged, share excursion details with someone at home, and use established tour operators for the village and island days.

In Casco Viejo, enjoy the busy tourist core and Uber back to Sortis instead of wandering outward late at night.
